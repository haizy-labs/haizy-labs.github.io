Security Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Reporting a Security Vulnerability

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability in Mermaid Editor Pro, please report it to:

Email: jiuzhouhai@gmail.com

We ask that you do not publicly disclose the issue until we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it.

Response Process

We aim to:

  • Acknowledge reports within 3 business days
  • Investigate reported vulnerabilities promptly
  • Provide updates during the investigation
  • Release fixes as quickly as practical

Data Protection

Mermaid Editor Pro for Confluence is built on the Atlassian Forge platform. For our other platform extensions (VS Code, JetBrains, Figma), refer to their respective documentation.

The app does not collect or store customer data outside Atlassian services unless explicitly documented in the Privacy Policy.

Responsible Disclosure

We appreciate responsible disclosure and will work with researchers to validate and resolve legitimate security issues.
